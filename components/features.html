Why use MARC?

A closer connection to your Mac.

Made for your iPhone

More than a quick system check.

See how your Mac is running, access your files, and handle everyday tasks from one app.

Save your servers and make the workspace yours with appearance, accent color, and refresh settings.

Monitor your system.

Check CPU, memory, storage, battery, and network activity. Explore usage over time and see which processes are using resources.

Work with files.

Browse folders, preview files, and upload or download through SFTP. Follow transfers as they happen.

Run commands.

Switch between SSH terminal sessions. Describe a task to AI, review the suggested command, and choose whether to run it.

Adjust your controls.

Change volume, mute audio, select an audio output, and adjust brightness. Manage Wi-Fi and Bluetooth from your phone.
